Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This explains what Vice.ai collects and why. We keep it to what the service needs.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and login, stored with Supabase.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number; we keep your plan, subscription status and Stripe customer reference.
  • Usage: live hours and clips used each month, so your plan limits work.
  • Your clips and settings: the streamers you add, the clips made for you and your app settings, stored on our servers (Modal) so you can use them in your browser.

How we use it

To run your account, make your clips, enforce plan limits, bill you through Stripe and answer support emails. Short pieces of stream audio and text are processed by our AI provider (Anthropic) to pick moments and write hooks. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run Vice.ai: Supabase (accounts and database), Stripe (payments), Modal (servers and storage) and Anthropic (AI), each for that purpose only. We may share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Finished clips may be deleted after 14 days of inactivity; download the ones you want to keep. Account data is kept while you have an account. Email us to delete your account and data.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing us. Cookies are used only to keep you signed in.
